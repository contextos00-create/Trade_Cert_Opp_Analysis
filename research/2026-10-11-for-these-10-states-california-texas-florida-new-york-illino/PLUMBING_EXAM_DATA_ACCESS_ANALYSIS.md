# A plumbing exam-prep app is plausible, but the research has not yet secured its content

Prepared October 11, 2026, for the proposed jurisdiction-specific plumbing certification preparation app.

Companion deliverable: [Modified data acquisition workflow](PLUMBING_DATA_ACQUISITION_WORKFLOW_V2.md).

## Executive summary

**Continue the idea, with a change in the acquisition strategy.** The strongest route supported by this investigation is to verify each specific exam's public blueprint and reference requirements, then obtain licensed practice content or commission original, expert-reviewed questions. The saved research did not obtain a commercially reusable official question bank. Publicly accessible exam information and permission to reuse content are separate results.

- **The original ten-state run was incomplete:** it stopped after 16 rounds, marked only 1 of 6 criteria met, and left 13 of 20 follow-up questions open. It recorded 239 source URLs, 41 marked read, and 50 findings. Its own authority/vendor coverage named five states; its code-edition coverage named three. These are discovery measures, not app readiness scores.
- **Several findings need correction or qualification:** Texas written exams now use Pearson VUE; California C-36 and the Florida state track are contractor credentials; New York City and Pennsylvania require local records; some official documents disagree about counts, logistics or reference editions.
- **Useful public material is obtainable:** the supplemental review found detailed outlines and reference lists for previously missing jurisdictions, including Georgia and Ohio, and a detailed NYC Master Plumber bulletin. Florida offers drawing criteria and dated reference lists. None establishes this project's rights to redistribute a live exam bank.
- **The next useful milestone is one complete exam package:** verified identity/format/edition, a documented content-use route, and a reviewed practice corpus. Treat specifications, rights and questions as three independent readiness gates. Expand the ten-state inventory through repeatable packages rather than treating one state as one exam.

The report does not establish demand, willingness to pay, licensing prices, or predictive validity of practice scores. Those require separate customer and pilot evidence.

## 2. What “access to test data” means for this app

There are several distinct assets, with different acquisition paths.

| Asset | What this review found | Practical acquisition route |
|---|---|---|
| Exam identity, rules and content outline | Frequently public, but sometimes incomplete, old or inconsistent. | Retrieve official board/vendor documents; verify exact credential/component/date. |
| Public sample questions | Limited examples appear in several guides; too few to establish a full bank. | Use for understanding exam style; obtain an appropriate reuse basis before copying into the app. |
| Live official questions and answer keys | No publicly downloadable, commercially reusable bank established; some authorities expressly restrict release/reproduction. | Ask about an approved practice-content partnership or releasable samples; do not make live-bank access the launch dependency. |
| Codebooks, tables, diagrams and commentary | Public viewing, paid books and subscriptions exist; commercial app rights are a separate question. | Publisher license/appropriate use review; original explanations and diagrams where supported. |
| Existing commercial practice banks | Prep providers are visible, but no license has been obtained by this project. | Evaluate content and negotiate a specific digital-content agreement. |
| Original practice questions | No original reviewed corpus was delivered by the research run. | Commission qualified authors, map to verified objectives, obtain necessary rights and independently review. |
| Exam outcomes/performance data | No validation dataset linking app practice to official exam results was established. | Request releasable aggregate statistics and later collect appropriately consented pilot outcomes. |

**Access to a blueprint is enough to begin a content-development brief. It is not enough to claim that an app uses official questions or predicts passing.**

Examples of the distinction are explicit: CSLB does not release actual test questions/keys for review; the Texas candidate handbook also restricts candidate review of actual questions; NYC's bulletin identifies the Department as question owner and requires permission for reproduction. [CSLB exam FAQ](https://cslb.ca.gov/About_us/Examination_FAQ.aspx), [Texas handbook](https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/494400.pdf), [NYC Master Plumber bulletin](https://cpshr.us/wp-content/uploads/NYCDOB-CIB__Master-Plumber-06.24.2025.pdf).

ICC's rules protect confidential examination material. Texas also has a test-item public-information exemption; a records request is therefore not a guaranteed route to live questions. Request existing public blueprints, released samples and aggregate data instead. These examples do not establish a universal legal rule for all ten states. [ICC examination rules](https://www.iccsafe.org/credentialing/admin-rules/), [Texas Attorney General Open Records Decision 626](https://www.texasattorneygeneral.gov/sites/default/files/ord-files/ord/2020/ord19940626.pdf).

### California: a relatively clear blueprint, for a contractor audience

The March 2026 C-36 guide allocates approximately 22% planning/estimating, 22% underground/rough systems, 20% finish installation, 19% service/repair/remodel, and 17% safety. It is closed book and contains three sample questions. Its resource list includes several 2025 California codes and other references. This supports a content map, but it does not settle exclusive tested-edition scope or sample reuse rights. [C-36 guide](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Resources/StudyGuides/C36StudyGuide.pdf).

**Implication:** a C-36 pilot is reasonable if the initial customer is pursuing contractor licensure. A trade-only product should say that explicitly; complete contractor preparation requires addressing Law and Business as applicable. Do not market this as a generic California journeyman exam.

### Texas: correct the vendor and reconcile official versions

The official Pearson program page was updated July 14, 2026. Its August 2025 handbook lists Tradesman written 85 items/120 minutes, Journeyman 100/120, and Master 308/360 plus a 30-minute break; it separately lists practical components for applicable routes. Scored versus unscored counts are not established here. The original research's PSI attribution and generic counts should not drive app configuration. [Official program](https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/tsbpe.html), [handbook](https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/494400.pdf).

The credential-specific September 2025 preparation guides distinguish Tradesman's 2018 IRC from Journeyman's 2018 IPC/IFGC or UPC. An older board Journeyman PDF says 90 written questions; a legacy Master PDF describes 210 multiple-choice questions plus other tasks, rather than the newer handbook's 308-item total. Do not guess how these counts reconcile. Ask TSBPE for the current section/count/scoring specification. [Journeyman guide](https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/494405.pdf), [Tradesman guide](https://www.pearsonvue.com/content/dam/VUE/vue/en/documents/publications/494406.pdf), [board forms](https://tsbpe.texas.gov/applications-forms/), [legacy Master guide](https://tsbpe.texas.gov/wp-content/uploads/documents/TSBPE_Master_ReviewCourse.pdf).

## 4. The content strategy should combine public specifications with original or licensed practice material

**Recommended default:** build the exam specification from official evidence, obtain lawful reference access for authors, and commission original questions/explanations/diagrams with qualified review. In parallel, evaluate licensing an existing practice bank. Released official examples are supplementary rather than the assumed foundation of a large corpus.

There is a concrete publisher route worth investigating: **ICC Code Connect** supplies code content through a licensed integration and requires implementation and content licensing agreements. That is a code-content route, not an official examination-question API. Confirm eligible product uses, exact state/title/edition coverage and commercial terms before designing around it. No price quote or agreement was obtained. [ICC Code Connect](https://solutions.iccsafe.org/codeconnect).

ICC's ordinary terms restrict uses beyond the granted license and direct further licensing inquiries to `license@iccsafe.org`. IAPMO's website terms reserve content rights. Buying a book, joining an organization or viewing a code page does not establish this app's copying/redistribution rights. [ICC terms](https://www.iccsafe.org/about/terms-of-use/), [IAPMO terms](https://iapmo.org/terms-of-use).

Legal text and publisher products also need careful classification: state/local government works are not automatically covered by the federal-government public-domain rule; official legal edicts have a separate copyright doctrine. Do not assume that every model code, commentary, index or figure shares the same status as an enacted ordinance. Review the particular material and intended use rather than treating either “government site” or “copyright notice” as a complete analysis. [USAGov guidance](https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright), [Copyright Office Compendium, §313.6(C)(2)](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/docs/compendium.pdf).

For licensing discussions, specify commercial digital display, excerpts, diagrams/tables, authoring/adaptation, storage, AI-assisted retrieval versus model training, translations, update rights and termination. Those are proposed questions for the rights holder, not permissions already established. For commissioned questions, require original authorship, the necessary rights, and a prohibition on contributing confidential recalled items.

## 5. Changes the app needs to reflect

### Route learners to the actual exam

Start with where the learner intends to obtain a credential/work, the credential they seek, their entry route and intended exam date. Residence/ZIP can suggest a location, but the learner should confirm the licensing authority and exam. One state may contain a statewide contractor program and multiple local worker credentials.

Store an exam package by **jurisdiction + official credential + exam/component ID + reference-version set + effective period**. Keep current adopted law, recommended study resources and explicitly tested references distinct. Apply local amendments to scored exam content only when the exam's scope supports that choice.

### Offer the right preparation modes

- Closed-book recall and timed practice for the relevant closed-book exam.
- Open-book lookup and reference-navigation exercises for permitted-reference exams.
- Original sizing, plan-reading and isometric exercises where tested.
- Separate business/law preparation for contractor packages when applicable.
- Procedure instruction and practical-task preparation for hands-on components, with clear limits on what a screen-based exercise assesses.

Use approved questions in scored mocks. AI can assist drafting from material the project may use, but must not invent code exceptions, answer keys or version applicability. Qualified review is particularly important for gas, safety, sizing, drawings and calculations.

### Start with learning evidence, then validate predictions

Report topic coverage, accuracy on reviewed items and time spent. A numeric “chance of passing” needs outcome validation tied to the same exam version, with sample size and repeat-attempt/exposure effects considered. The saved research and simulated repository scores provide no such evidence.

## 6. What to change in the research process and repository design

The repository's [nationwide build document](https://github.com/contextos00-create/Trade_Cert_Opp_Analysis/blob/c9fad3b34545337d2401a913de5dd76415edd149/NATIONWIDE_US_TRADE_CERTIFICATION_BUILD_DOC.md) already specifies credential-specific records, primary-source hierarchy, null unknowns, document hashes, precise citations, effective dates, contradictions, review gates and bounded retries. Reuse those principles. Add the missing content-rights and question-development stages, and narrow the immediate execution to plumbing exam packages.

The checked-in implementation is much smaller than the README's description:

- [run_lane.py](https://github.com/contextos00-create/Trade_Cert_Opp_Analysis/blob/c9fad3b34545337d2401a913de5dd76415edd149/run_lane.py) explicitly generates sample scraped text, hard-codes a mixed code cycle and passing score, and assigns availability/effort values from jurisdiction scope. Its 0.85/0.55 scores and 4.0/12.5 hours are simulated, not measured feasibility or effort. It does not demonstrate the workflow that generated the saved research run.
- [trade_schema.json](https://github.com/contextos00-create/Trade_Cert_Opp_Analysis/blob/c9fad3b34545337d2401a913de5dd76415edd149/trade_schema.json) lacks the necessary source/version evidence, exam components, rights and practice-item records. Mandatory feasibility fields should not force invented values when measurements are unavailable.
- [README](https://github.com/contextos00-create/Trade_Cert_Opp_Analysis/blob/c9fad3b34545337d2401a913de5dd76415edd149/README.md) describes planned directories, commands and features that are absent in this checkout. It should distinguish current prototype, saved research and intended architecture. Its MIT-license statement also does not confer rights in external codebooks or exam content; the referenced license file was absent in the audited snapshot.

Recommended process changes:

1. Run a jurisdiction/credential inventory, then dispatch one exact exam package per job.
2. Retrieve authoritative unread leads before broad searches; record fetch/parser failures and use a targeted alternate method.
3. Check each material claim's source, credential fit, passage support and effective date. Resolve provider and edition conflicts explicitly.
4. Keep a gap queue keyed to exam/component and missing field; use at most three total research passes per topic (initial pass plus two targeted retries), then resume when new evidence arrives.
5. Track **specification verified**, **content rights established**, and **practice content approved** independently.
6. Persist source versions and map changed fields to affected questions/forms. Do not automatically replace exam references when a building code changes.

The JSON settings say `maxMinutes: 60`, but the creation/finish timestamps span about 198 minutes. They do not establish active execution time or the cause of the discrepancy. The next implementation should enforce/checkpoint its runtime budget and report actual elapsed/tool outcomes rather than rely on prompt limits alone.

## 7. The next milestones and decision criteria

### First: close the acquisition question for one worker-focused exam

For the user's individual-tradesperson audience, **Georgia Journeyman is a strong candidate for the first content-feasibility package**: its retrieved 2026 bulletin provides a detailed outline, dated references and book rules. Washington journey-level and Texas Journeyman are reasonable alternatives, but have specific edition/version questions to close. This is a research-readiness judgment, not proof that Georgia has the best customer economics.

If contractor candidates are the intended first audience, consider **California C-36 or Ohio plumbing contractor** as separately scoped options and include the applicable business/law preparation. Do not choose the easiest public contractor data and present it as a worker-certification product.

### Then: run specification, rights and authoring work in parallel

- Retrieve and accept the exact current exam package; ask the regulator/vendor for written clarification only on gaps that remain.
- Request an appropriate publisher/practice-content proposal, specifying needed uses and editions. Request released examples rather than rely on secure live items.
- Prepare an original-authoring brief and cost estimate with a qualified instructor/reviewer. Start with a small formative sample before paying to build a complete bank.
- Test the sample's correctness, explanations and learner usefulness. Expand to weighted timed forms only after all necessary topics and assets are covered.

No emails, licensing requests, purchases or agreements were executed in preparing this report. The companion workflow supplies the stages, queue, output contracts, gates and reusable research prompt for that next execution.

### Continue, narrow, or stop based on observable outcomes

**Continue to a pilot** when the necessary exam specification is accepted, the content-use route is documented, approved items cover the stated scope, and the initial content/maintenance cost is acceptable to the project.

**Narrow the offering** to topic practice or a requirements guide when exact simulation fields or particular assets remain unavailable. Label editorial topic distributions and practical limits accurately.

**Change the acquisition route** if a requested bank is refused, unsuitable or unaffordable: compare original authoring, another licensed provider, or a smaller reference-light product. A refusal of live-bank access alone does not invalidate original practice preparation.

**Reconsider the product scope** if necessary current specifications cannot be confirmed, lawful content cannot be obtained/created, maintenance costs are excessive, or learners do not find the reviewed content useful. This investigation does not supply those cost or demand results yet.

## 8. Methods, provenance and limits

The original research narrative and full JSON were reviewed alongside README, `run_lane.py`, `trade_schema.json` and the nationwide design document at repository commit **`c9fad3b34545337d2401a913de5dd76415edd149`**. Run counts were derived from the JSON status, criteria, questions, sources and findings, not estimated from narrative length.

Supplemental verification used primary government, code-body and contracted exam-provider sources. Citation links identify the particular guide, bulletin or governing page. Date applicability is called out when a document is old, annual, proposed, future-effective or inconsistent with a current page. A still-linked old bulletin is evidence to evaluate, not proof of current applicability.

Illinois Part 750 was retrieved through direct HTTP after the browsing service failed; the actual official HTML was read. Arizona's live application page could not be read directly, so that blocked page's indexed text was not used as a sole launch-critical specification; the readable PSI documents and Phoenix government page support the reported paths. Official TAC access limits also prevent conclusively resolving any later Texas code adoption.

The local examples do not exhaust New York, Pennsylvania, Arizona or other local licensing overlays. No rights holder was contacted, no commercial rights granted, no live exam corpus acquired, no mock question bank created, and no learner outcomes analyzed. Accordingly, this is an evidence-based feasibility assessment and a modified acquisition process—not a launch-readiness certification.

The original run remains the historical record. This report identifies supplemental findings and proposed changes without rewriting its evidence or presenting those changes as implemented.
